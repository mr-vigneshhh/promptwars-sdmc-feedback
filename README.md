# promptwars-sdmc-feedback
My personal feedback, experience, suggestions, and learnings from the PromptWars × SDMC Hackathon 2026.

ElectionFlow — Hackathon Feedback

About

ElectionFlow was built during the PromptWars × SDMC Hackathon on 17 September 2026 at SDM College, Ujire.

The goal was simple — make the election process easier to understand through a clear and user-friendly web experience.

My Experience

This hackathon was more than just building a project for me.

I had limited time, pressure, and the challenge of turning an idea into a working website. Seeing ElectionFlow reach Rank #1 on the dashboard and later enter the Top 10 stage was a very proud moment for me.

For someone who is still learning and improving every day, that moment gave me a lot of confidence.

My Suggestions

For future hackathons, I sincerely suggest:

- Clearly explain the evaluation criteria before judging.
- Maintain consistent judging standards for all participants.
- Give participants some feedback after the final round.
- Make the award and recognition criteria more transparent.

I believe these small changes can make the experience better and more meaningful for every participant.

My Disappointment

I won't hide the fact that I was disappointed.

Reaching Rank #1 on the dashboard and making it to the Top 10 stage made me believe that my hard work had a chance of being recognized.

When I finished my final presentation without receiving an award or specific recognition, it was difficult to accept.

Still, I respect the organizers and judges, and I understand that competitions have their own evaluation process.

I only hope that in future events, participants receive clearer feedback so they can understand where they did well and where they can improve.

Final Note

Whatever the result, ElectionFlow is something I am personally proud of.

I built it under pressure, learned new things, stood on the stage, presented my work, and gave my best.

Maybe I didn't take home a trophy this time.

But I am taking the experience, the lessons, and the confidence with me. ❤️‍🩹
